COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

How ClearPro Lab Compares

ClearPro vs. Arch for Labs

While Arch for Labs excels at QR-based bench tracking, ClearPro Lab solves the Efficiency Paradox—where production is fast but communication is slow. Our unified AI-messaging ecosystem bridges the gap between the lab floor and the clinic front-office.

ClearPro vs. Legacy Systems (Magic Touch / DLCPM)

Legacy ERP systems like Magic Touch or LabStar can be complex and costly. ClearPro Lab offers a cloud-native, agile alternative with faster implementation and intuitive AI automation, making it the preferred choice for modern digital-first laboratories.

Common questions

Quick answers about ClearPro Tech LLC and ClearPro Lab.

ClearPro Tech LLC is a United States-based artificial intelligence and healthcare technology company that builds AI platforms, digital dentistry software, and enterprise automation systems.
ClearPro Lab is ClearPro Tech's cloud-based digital dental laboratory management platform. It connects clinics, laboratories, and teams through case management, AI-assisted messaging, and workflow automation.
Unlike legacy systems like Magic Touch (DLCPM) or bench-tracking tools like Arch for Labs, ClearPro Lab provides a modern, cloud-native messaging ecosystem. It resolves the "Efficiency Paradox" by integrating administrative communication directly into the production workflow with AI-powered prescription reading and automated messaging.
Existing clinic and lab partners can sign in directly at clearprolab.com using the Client Portal link in the navigation or the ClearPro Lab section above.
