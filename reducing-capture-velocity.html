What is Capture Velocity?

In the digital dental pipeline, Capture Velocity refers to the speed and efficiency with which a dental laboratory can "capture" a case from a clinic and move it into the production workflow. It is the critical metric that determines the true lead time of a dental restoration.

The Capture Velocity Equation:
T (Total Time) = T (Scanning) + T (Transmission) + T (Intake/Processing) + T (Production)

While most labs have optimized the Production phase (milling, 3D printing), the Intake phase remains a bottleneck. High capture velocity means cases are stuck in "administrative limbo," waiting for manual data entry, prescription verification, and file organization.

The Bottleneck: Manual Case Intake

Traditional labs lose hours every day to:

  • Manually reading physical and digital prescriptions.
  • Correcting data entry errors from intraoral scan exports.
  • Managing disjointed communication across email, WhatsApp, and phone calls.

This "administrative friction" reduces the lab's overall velocity, regardless of how fast their milling machines are.
