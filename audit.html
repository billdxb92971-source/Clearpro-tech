What is Capture Velocity in Dental Labs?

Capture velocity measures the speed and accuracy with which a dental lab receives, processes, and prepares digital scans for production. In today's highly competitive market, high capture velocity is the cornerstone of digital workflow efficiency. When technicians or administrative staff spend 15 to 30 minutes manually downloading intraoral scans, matching prescriptions, and organizing patient folders, your capture velocity drops to critical levels.

By automating these front-end processes, labs drastically improve their automated case intake ROI. High capture velocity means cases move from the doctor's scanner directly into your design software's queue in seconds, not hours. This operational speed ensures faster turnaround times, happier clinicians, and optimized infrastructure.

The Economic Cost of Manual Scan Intake

Every minute spent on manual file management translates directly to lost revenue. Utilizing our dental lab ROI calculator reveals a startling reality for many growing businesses: manual case entry is scaling costs linearly alongside growth. If a lab receives 1,000 cases per month and spends just 10 minutes manually processing each one, that equals over 160 hours of labor monthly—equivalent to a full-time employee doing nothing but data entry.

The true dental lab labor cost isn't just the hourly wage; it's the lost opportunity. Highly skilled CAD technicians should be designing restorations, not hunting for missing STL files or clarifying handwritten Rx forms. Upgrading your systems to achieve high automated case intake ROI eliminates this bottleneck, turning administrative waste into actionable production capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to reduce dental lab administrative costs?

The most effective way to reduce dental lab administrative costs is by eliminating manual data entry through automated intake portals. By integrating your scanners and design software, you can remove the need for staff to manually download files and input prescriptions, instantly improving your digital workflow efficiency.

How much does manual data entry cost a dental lab?

Depending on case volume and staff hourly rates, manual data entry can cost a mid-sized dental lab tens of thousands of dollars annually. As shown in our dental lab labor cost calculator, spending just 15 minutes per case can quickly amount to massive annual labor waste that could otherwise be invested in production or new technology.
