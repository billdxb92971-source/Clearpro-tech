Dental LMS AI Automation & Intraoral Scan API

Enable high-velocity AI automation in your lab environment by connecting your Dental Lab Management Software (LMS) directly to the ClearPro Engine. Our API is engineered for product managers and CTOs looking to eliminate manual prescription entry and standardize intraoral scan data.

Technical Specifications

  • Base Endpoint: https://api.clearprotech.com/v1/
  • Authentication: Bearer Token (HMAC-SHA256)
  • Data Format: JSON-based structured dental prescription data
  • Sandbox Environment: Fully functional sandbox available for development and testing of EasyRx and GreatLab integration flows.

Key Integration Capabilities

  • GreatLab Webhook Integration: Listen for real-time order status updates and case intake events to trigger instant AI processing.
  • EasyRx AI Automation: Map incoming EasyRx prescriptions directly into our AI-driven processing pipeline, ensuring 100% data consistency.
  • Intraoral Scan API: Standardize and parse raw intraoral scan metadata, reducing manual intervention in your lab’s digital workflow.
  • Real-time Prescription Parsing: Trigger automated AI reading the moment a case is created in your LMS.
