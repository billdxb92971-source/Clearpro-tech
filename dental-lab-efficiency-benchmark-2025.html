Understanding the Efficiency Paradox in High-Volume Labs

As dental labs scale, they often encounter the 'Efficiency Paradox': adding more staff and equipment doesn't always correlate with increased throughput. In many cases, it creates bottlenecks. We are conducting a 2025 research study to map this paradox and identify the true drivers of dental lab efficiency.
