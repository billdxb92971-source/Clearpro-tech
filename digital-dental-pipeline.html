9-Stage AI Pipeline

The definitive framework for modern dental laboratory automation.

Zero-Touch Case Intake

At the core of the ClearPro ecosystem is Zero-Touch Case Intake. By leveraging proprietary AI-powered OCR, we eliminate the manual bottleneck where staff spend hours re-typing clinic data. Our "Universal Intake" engine pulls from iTero, Medit, 3Shape, and Carestream portals simultaneously.

01. INTAKE Automated case reception. Physical prescriptions are digitized using AI-powered OCR (Zero-Touch) to eliminate manual entry errors.
02. SCANNING & TRIAGE Validation of STL files and digital impressions. AI flags missing data or poor scan quality immediately before bench work begins.
03. AI ANALYSIS Predictive analysis of case complexity and resource allocation, ensuring the right technician handles the right case.
04. DESIGN (CAD) Digital design of restorations. Seamless integration with leading CAD software like 3Shape and exocad.
05. MANUFACTURING (CAM) Milling or 3D printing of the designed dental restorations using high-precision automated systems.
06. FINISHING Manual post-processing, staining, and glazing by skilled technicians to ensure aesthetic excellence.
07. QUALITY CONTROL AI-assisted verification against the original digital prescription and clinical requirements.
08. LOGISTICS Automated shipping labels, real-time tracking, and delivery notifications sent directly to the dental clinic.
09. FEEDBACK LOOP Closing the loop with clinician feedback and performance data to refine future designs and workflows.
