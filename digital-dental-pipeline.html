The 9-Stage Digital Dental Pipeline

The definitive framework for modern dental laboratory automation, powered by ClearPro Lab. Our digital dentistry workflow software is designed to improve lab capture velocity and operational efficiency.

The 9 Stages

  • Stage 1: Zero-Touch Case Intake - Leveraging proprietary AI-powered OCR to eliminate manual re-typing.
  • Stage 2: Scan Validation & Triage - Automated AI checks for STL file margin clarity.
  • Stage 3: AI Case Analysis - Predictive analysis of case complexity for resource allocation.
  • Stage 4: Automated CAD Design - AI-driven morphology generation integrated with 3Shape and exocad.
  • Stage 5: Manufacturing (CAM) - High-precision milling and 3D printing systems.
  • Stage 6: Skilled Finishing - Human-centric aesthetic refinement.
  • Stage 7: AI Quality Control - Predictive verification against clinical prescriptions.
  • Stage 8: Logistics Automation - Real-time tracking and automated shipping.
  • Stage 9: Performance Feedback Loop - Data-driven refinement for future workflows.
