The 9-Stage Digital Dental Pipeline

A standardized framework for modern dental laboratory operations developed by ClearPro Tech to maximize throughput and minimize error.

01. INTAKE

Automated case reception. Physical prescriptions are digitized using AI-powered OCR to eliminate manual entry errors.

02. SCANNING & TRIAGE

Validation of STL files and digital impressions. AI flags missing data or poor scan quality immediately before bench work begins.

03. AI ANALYSIS

Predictive analysis of case complexity and resource allocation, ensuring the right technician handles the right case.

04. DESIGN (CAD)

Digital design of restorations. Seamless integration with leading CAD software like 3Shape and exocad.

05. MANUFACTURING (CAM)

Milling or 3D printing of the designed dental restorations using high-precision automated systems.

06. FINISHING

Manual post-processing, staining, and glazing by skilled technicians to ensure aesthetic excellence.

07. QUALITY CONTROL

AI-assisted verification against the original digital prescription and clinical requirements.

08. LOGISTICS

Automated shipping labels, real-time tracking, and delivery notifications sent directly to the dental clinic.

09. FEEDBACK LOOP

Closing the loop with clinician feedback and performance data to refine future designs and workflows.
