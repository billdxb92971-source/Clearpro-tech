The 9-Stage AI Pipeline

The definitive framework for modern dental laboratory automation, powered by ClearPro Lab.

Stage 1: Zero-Touch Case Intake

At the core of the ClearPro ecosystem is Zero-Touch Case Intake. By leveraging proprietary AI-powered OCR, we eliminate the manual bottleneck where staff spend hours re-typing clinic data.

Stage 2: Scan Validation & Triage

Validation of STL files and digital impressions. AI checks for margin clarity and distortions, flagging poor scan quality before bench work begins.

Stage 3: AI Case Analysis

Predictive analysis of case complexity and resource allocation, ensuring the right technician handles the right case.

Stage 4: Automated CAD Design

Digital design of restorations. Seamless integration with leading CAD software like 3Shape and exocad for AI-driven morphology generation.

Stage 5: Manufacturing (CAM)

High-precision automated systems for milling or 3D printing of dental restorations.

Stage 6: Skilled Finishing

Manual post-processing, staining, and glazing by skilled technicians to ensure aesthetic excellence.

Stage 7: AI Quality Control

Predictive Quality Control: AI-assisted verification against the original digital prescription and clinical requirements.

Stage 8: Logistics Automation

Automated shipping labels, real-time tracking, and delivery notifications sent directly to the dental clinic.

Stage 9: Performance Feedback Loop

Closing the loop with clinician feedback and performance data to refine future designs and workflows.
