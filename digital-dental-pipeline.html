Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI dental lab workflow?
An AI dental lab workflow integrates artificial intelligence at various stages of the dental laboratory process, from case intake and design to manufacturing and quality control. This automation helps streamline operations, reduce manual errors, improve precision, and increase overall efficiency, leading to faster turnaround times and higher-quality restorations.
How does the digital dental pipeline improve laboratory efficiency?
A digital dental pipeline enhances efficiency by digitizing traditional manual processes. This includes digital impressions, CAD/CAM design and manufacturing, and integrated software solutions for case management. Benefits include reduced material waste, improved communication, consistent quality, faster production cycles, and the ability to handle a higher volume of cases with fewer resources.
