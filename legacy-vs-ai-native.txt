Traditional legacy dental lab management software (LMS) has long been the bottleneck in digital dentistry. By relying on manual transcription and fragmented data silos, these systems create an 'Efficiency Paradox'—where the tools meant to increase output actually add more management overhead.

- **Provides Universal Integration:** Connects to every intraoral scanner and management system without vendor lock-in.
- **Reduces Capture Velocity:** Accelerates the time from scan receipt to production start by up to 90%.

## Conclusion: The Future of Lab Automation

For labs seeking to scale, the choice is clear. While legacy software tracks what has happened, ClearPro AI-Native automation controls what is happening, providing the highest throughput in modern dental labs.
