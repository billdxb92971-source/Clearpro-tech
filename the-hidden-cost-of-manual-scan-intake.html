The Hidden Cost of Manual Scan Intake: How Labs are Losing 15% Margin

The Efficiency Gap in Digital Dentistry

Every minute a technician spends manually reviewing a scan, fixing margin lines, or clarifying prescription data is a minute stolen from production. In today's digital workflow, manual intake is the single biggest bottleneck.

What is AI Scan Routing?

AI scan routing uses intelligent algorithms to automatically categorize, pre-screen, and route digital dental impressions the moment they arrive. Instead of a human opening every file, the system instantly flags missing margins or insufficient preparation data.

Why You're Losing 15% Margin

The cost of manual intake goes beyond labor. It includes the downstream impact of remakes caused by human error during the intake phase. Automated, AI-driven workflows recover this lost margin by ensuring only high-quality data reaches your design team.
